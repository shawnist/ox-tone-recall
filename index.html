Start
  1. Select a guitar track in LUNA. It appears here in a dashed row marked Not linked.
  2. Press Link track on that row. The track joins the list, and the sound the OX is on is saved as that track's sound.
  3. Record. Every clip recorded on that track keeps the sound it was recorded with. Keep this panel open while you record.

Dial in sounds in the OX app as usual. This panel never changes your OX unless you press Recall.

The lamp

On the OXThe OX is on exactly this sound.

ChangedYou recalled this sound, then changed something on the OX.

UnlitThe OX is on something else.

The lamp compares the actual settings, not a name, so two sounds only match when they really are the same.

The two buttons

Recall puts a track's or a clip's sound on the OX. It is the only thing here that changes the OX, and it is off while recording.

Save to track keeps what the OX is on now as the sound of the track selected in LUNA. It appears only when the OX isn't already on that track's sound. LUNA's Undo reverses it.

Clips

Click a track's name to open it and see its clips. Selecting a clip in LUNA opens its track for you.

A clip's sound is what the OX was on when it was recorded, and it never changes. Press its Recall to get that sound back, for example before punching in.

To make a clip's sound the track's sound, Recall the clip, then press Save to track.

OX was offline means the OX couldn't be reached during that recording, so no sound was saved with it.

OX Link, and reaching your OX

Inside LUNA this panel reaches your OX through OX Link, a small app that comes with this add-on and sits in the Mac's menu bar. LUNA only lets add-ons talk to this Mac, so without OX Link the panel can't see the OX.

If the red line says OX Link isn't running:

The first time, macOS asks whether OX Link may find devices on your local network. Click Allow. OX Link starts at login after that (its menu has Start at Login), and this panel connects by itself within a few seconds.

OX Link is running but the panel still can't reach the OX? Check the OX is switched on and on the same network as this Mac. OX Link's menu shows the OX it has found. If you clicked Don't Allow, turn OX Link on in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.

More

What changed appears under a Changed lamp and lists which parts of the OX differ, such as EQ or Cab and mics.

Unlink a track: click the track's name to open it, then press Unlink track from OX Recall. Its saved sounds are kept.
